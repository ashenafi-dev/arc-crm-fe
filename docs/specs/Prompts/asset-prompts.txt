HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
1. Generate each asset with the prompt in its section (Midjourney, DALL·E,
   Firefly, Ideogram, Leonardo, etc.). Every prompt already includes the shared
   style, colours and background rules, so each one can be pasted on its own.
2. Remove the background if the tool didn't (remove.bg, Photoshop "Remove
   Background", Figma, Canva). Every asset must be a transparent PNG.
3. Save it with the exact file name below into the exact folder. The site
   picks it up automatically and the dashed placeholder disappears. No code
   changes are needed.
4. Export at 2x size (the "Export" line in each asset gives the target) and
   compress with TinyPNG or Squoosh so each file stays under ~400 KB.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 WHY THESE ASSETS EXIST (the short version)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The site is a procurement and labor-approval system for an architecture firm:
someone requests materials or a crew, Finance checks the budget, the General
Manager confirms the need, the Owner gives final sign-off, and everything is
logged. The 3D objects make that abstract flow feel physical: blueprints,
materials, stamps, approval seals and a hard hat say "construction + paperwork
+ sign-off" at a glance. The role portraits put a real face on each person in
the chain so visitors immediately see "this is for my team". Together they
give the pages life and teach the domain without extra text.

metal, paper and blueprint textures. No text, no logos, no clutter.

People: real photographic portraits, not illustrations. Ethiopian men and
women with authentic Ethiopian features, a range of ages and genders, each
with a distinct and memorable face. Waist-up, three-quarter angle, looking
slightly toward the left side of the frame, where the card text sits. Shallow
depth of field, soft cinematic key light with warm rim light, real skin
texture. Outfits are modern professional workwear that fits each job (safety vest,
blazer, hard hat, lanyard), not traditional or cultural dress. Only the people
are Ethiopian. Brand colours appear as small accents.
Cleanly cut out, with the bottom edge cropped straight at the waist so they
"stand" on the card's bottom edge.

 ROLE PORTRAITS — shared rules for assets 5 to 9
================================================================================
Where:  Home page, "Built for everyone who touches a purchase." Five tall
        cards side by side, each about 480 px tall. The role name and a short
        description sit at the top; the person fills the bottom and bleeds
        slightly up behind the text and out to the card edges.

        their own team, and shows who approves what.
Frame:  Portrait 3:4. Waist-up, head in the upper-middle third, shoulders
        wide, body cropped straight at the waist along the bottom edge. Body
        turned three-quarters, face toward the LEFT of the frame (toward the
        card text). Leave clear space above the head.
Export: 1500 x 2000 px, transparent PNG. Clean hair edges (no halo), because
        some cards are dark and some are light.
Set:    Shoot all five with the same lens (85 mm), the same light direction
        (key light from upper left, warm rim light from right) and the same
        camera height so they look like one photo series.
