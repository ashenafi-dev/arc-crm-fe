WHAT THE LOGO HAS TO SAY
------------------------
Arch Ops is the procurement and labor-approval system for an architecture
firm. A request goes Finance -> General Manager -> Owner, and every step is
signed off and logged. The logo should feel like three things at once:
  - Architecture: structure, arches, blueprints, precise geometry
  - Approval: a checkmark, a seal, "signed off"
  - Flow: steps moving forward in order
It should look confident and bold, like the site: black, heavy type, one hot
red accent and a touch of sun yellow.


BRAND COLOURS (use only these)
------------------------------
  Signal red   #EA4B2C   primary mark colour
  Ink black    #181412   warm near-black, for type and outlines
  Sun yellow   #F4C534   small accent only (a dot, a check, a corner)
  Warm canvas  #F3F1EE   the light background the logo usually sits on
  White        #FFFFFF   for the reversed version on red or black

Font the site uses: Onest (Black/ExtraBold for the wordmark). Type the name
yourself in Onest after generating the symbol. AI tools often misspell text.


LOGO RULES (every prompt already follows these)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Flat vector style: solid shapes, no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no
   textures, no photo effects.
2. Simple enough to read at 16 px (favicon) and 32 px (sidebar tile).
   At most 2 to 3 shapes in the symbol.
3. Works in one colour: it must still read in all-black and all-white.
4. Geometric and precise: built on a grid, consistent stroke weights, round
   or square ends applied the same way everywhere.
5. Max 2 brand colours in the main version (red + ink); yellow only as a
   tiny accent.
6. No clip-art clichés: no house icon, no generic crane, no globe, no
   shaking hands, no swooshes.
7. Centred with generous clear space around it.
8. Transparent background for the main version. Solid backgrounds are only
   for the alternate "app icon" versions (red tile or ink tile), noted per
   prompt.

Negative prompt (add wherever the tool supports it):
  gradient, 3D, bevel, shadow, glow, texture, photo, realistic, mockup,
  paper, wall, busy background, multiple logos, grid of options, clip art,
  house icon, globe, handshake, swoosh, watercolor, sketch, extra text,
  misspelled text, watermark

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO PICK THE WINNER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Shrink it to 16 px: can you still tell what it is?
[ ] Turn it all-black and all-white: does it still work?
[ ] Put it on the warm canvas (#F3F1EE) and on the dark sidebar (#1D1A18)
[ ] Does it say architecture + approval without explaining it?
[ ] Would it look good stamped on an approved purchase request?
[ ] Is it different enough from generic "construction company" logos?
Tip: Options 1 and 3 usually win for small sizes. Option 2 or 5 pairs
nicely with them as the full logo for the header.
